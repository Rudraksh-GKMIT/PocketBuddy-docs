## Introduction

**PocketBuddy** is a simple and efficient **family expense tracking system** that allows households to collaboratively manage and monitor their financial activities.  
Instead of focusing on individual users we focused on **family-based structure**, where the **admin** manages family members and oversees shared financial data.

The system enables:
- The **admin** to create accounts for family members.  
- Each **member** to log in and manually record their expenses.  
- The system to generate a **combined and categorized family expense overview**.

---

## Why Choose It

PocketBuddy addresses a common household problem the lack of visibility into overall family spending.  
Many families use manual logs to track expenses, making it difficult to get a unified picture of household finances.  

PocketBuddy simplifies this by:
- Centralizing all expenses under one family account.  
- Allowing members to record transactions in real-time.  
- Giving the the family clear insights into where money is spent.  
- Promoting **financial collaboration and awareness** within the family.
